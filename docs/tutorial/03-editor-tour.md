## Logging in

Go to [scenex.org](https://scenex.org) and log in with your email address.
Either ask for a **magic link** (an email with a login button — no password
needed) or use your password if you've set one.

![The login page](images/login.png)

New to Scenex? You can't sign up on your own — the owner of a scenario
**invites** you. The invitation email contains a link that creates your
account and takes you straight to the scenario.

## Your scenarios

After logging in you see **Games**: every scenario you're working on.

![The scenario list](images/scenarios.png)

Click **Open editor** to work on a scenario. (You can also start a new one
from scratch here — type a name, pick the language you'll write in, and click
**Create scenario**.)

## The editor at a glance

The editor has a row of tabs — one per building block. Here is Common
Ground's **Settings** tab:

![Common Ground's Settings tab](images/editor-settings.png)

| Tab | What lives there | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| **Settings** | name, description, director's notes for the whole show, visibility, language | below |
| **Values** | what the game measures | [4](04-values.md) |
| **Overall index** | the headline score (Common Ground: the Democracy Score) | [4](04-values.md) |
| **Groups** | the teams | [5](05-groups.md) |
| **Initial** | every group's starting numbers | [5](05-groups.md) |
| **Timeline** | Events, Votes and Wildcards, with their options | [6](06-timeline.md) |
| **Labels** | coloured tags for options | [7](07-pages-and-more.md) |
| **Endings** | the final scenes | [7](07-pages-and-more.md) |
| **Pages** | full-screen slides for the projector | [7](07-pages-and-more.md) |
| **Media** | pictures, video and sound | [7](07-pages-and-more.md) |
| **Members** | who else may work on it (owner only) | [7](07-pages-and-more.md) |

Up in the right-hand corner: **Dry run** lets you play the scenario on your
own ([chapter 9](09-dry-run.md)); **Sessions** is where performances begin
([chapter 10](10-sessions.md)).

The **Working locale** row (`en de es et hu …`) switches the language you are
editing — more in [Your Common Ground](08-your-common-ground.md).

## Three habits that save you trouble

1. **Every tab has its own Save button.** Nothing is stored until you click
   it.
2. **Long texts are Markdown.** Write `**bold**` for **bold**, `*italic*`
   for *italic*, start a line with `- ` for a bullet point. Pictures from the
   media library can go right into the text.
3. **Handles stay in English.** They're the internal names everybody uses to
   find things — translations go into the separate text fields.

## Settings

- **Handle** — the internal name (Common Ground's is `COMMON GROUND`).
- **Name, Tagline, Description** — how you present the scenario.
- **Director's notes** — notes for the game master about the whole show: the
  run sheet, the arc, timing.
- **Visibility** — *Draft* (only members see it), *Invite only*, or
  *Published* (every logged-in user can look at it and try a dry run; only
  members can change it).
- **Source locale** — the language the scenario was written in. Missing
  translations fall back to it.
- **Change highlight** — how many seconds a changed number stays marked on
  the screens after a decision.
