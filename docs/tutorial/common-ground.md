# Appendix: Common Ground at a glance

[← Show-day checklist](13-show-day.md) · [Contents](index.md) · [Complete content →](common-ground-content.md)

*COMMON GROUND – The Art of Compromise* is a participatory theatre
simulation about democratic decision-making under pressure. It's the running
example of this tutorial.

![Common Ground](images/display-page-title.png)

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Players** | 30–60, all players, no passive audience |
| **Duration** | about 3 hours, one continuous timeline |
| **Setting** | the town of Lux |
| **Question** | not who wins — whether a democratic community can hold together under pressure |

## The story

Lux has an old **Hat Factory** — and every group gets to decide what to build
in its part of it. Then a **mineral spring** is discovered underneath. An
**international IT company** takes notice: first with an *irresistible
proposal*, then with an *incomplete truth* about its data centre plans, and
finally at the table for a **water development pact**. In between, the town
elects its representative, and the candidates campaign on **social media**
and **climate change** policies — decided in **referendums** by the whole
room.

## The building blocks in Common Ground

| Block | In Common Ground |
|---|---|
| Groups | Chamber of Commerce · Arts, Culture, Sport · Customer's Protection · Voluntary Sector · Public Services |
| Values | Stability, Solidarity, Prosperity, Fairness (0–20, global = average); everyone starts at 8 |
| Overall Index | Democracy Score = average of the four; *Breakdown · Critical · Fragile · Resilient · In Bloom* |
| Well-being | Well-Being: 🙁 Unhappy · 😐 Ok · 🙂 Happy · 😀 Very happy |
| Events | *Choose your slogan!*, Key Events 1–5, the campaign events |
| Two versions | Key Events 3, 4 (higher / lower Democracy Score) and 5 (high / medium / low) |
| Votes | Referendum 1 (the representative), 2 (social media policy), 3 (climate change policy) |
| Wildcards | a Freeze Card per group; *City of Lux receives an investment!* |
| Pages | title, *Welcome to Lux*, *Happiness Survey*, *Survey at the End*, three finales |

The [complete content](common-ground-content.md) — every option and every
number — is generated straight from the scenario.
